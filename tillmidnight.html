TILL MIDNIGHT

Summer 2023-Spring 2025
Product Manager/Designer/Writer
Genre: Science-Fiction/Alternate History/Simulation

Synopsis: 
Till Midnight is a 2D side-scrolling narrative simulation game. You play as a commanding officer navigating a darkly atmospheric submarine amid critical communication failures. Manage time-sensitive tasks to keep your submarine's critical systems operational and make a final decision with world-shattering consequences.

Inspired by true historical events. Spoilers below.

In 2025, the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists set the Doomsday Clock to 89 seconds to midnight, the closest it has ever been since its inception in 1947. The only notion that stands between humanity and the apocalypse is the pretext of M.A.D (Mutually Assured Destruction).

I was part of a project to design a game that brought awareness to what is arguably one of the greatest existential threats to humanity today, the proliferation of nuclear weapons.

emotion by design

Till Midnight began with a simple emotional promise, to design a game that created the ideal conditions for evoking the fullest extent of the human capacity for stress.

It revels in the gripping stories of Vasili Arkhipov and Stanislav Petrov, two men both hailed as 'the man who saved the world' for their individual actions that single-handedly averted full-scale nuclear conflict between the United States and the Soviet Union during the Cold War.

​Few other stressful premises could top that.

the challenge

The goal of this project was to represent the stories of Arkhipov and Petrov by infusing the game with real-world situational elements that evoke a sustained sense of stress in the player. The game needed to wear its influences on its sleeve, to deliver an authentic, narratively driven gut-punch at the moment of catharsis, one that would leave space for candid reflection on the impact of nuclear weapons once the experience had concluded.

HIGH LEVEL GOALS

1. Create a high-pressure game loop the sustains a sense of stress in the player
2. Develop an immersive, highly atmospheric environment that induces psychological horror
3. Heavy subject matter requires a reflective narrative approach to avoid triviality

MY ROLE

Till Midnight started development in Unity during the summer of 2023, where I took on the position of Product Manager, Designer and Writer. I managed a technical team that developed a custom engine as part of course project requirements, alongside a fellow Designer. In my capacity as a Product Manager, I developed detailed project timelines and held weekly stand-ups to check in on progress. An early build of the game was ported over to the custom engine, before a team handover that resulted in the completion of the final build during Spring 2025.

project kickoff

At the beginning of the project, we conducted research on the target market and audience for the game, creating user personas to gain insights into player behaviour and market trends. We discovered that simulation was an emerging platform within the sci-fi genre, popularized by games such as Among Us.

​To differentiate ourselves in the market, we combined the appeal of the simulation genre with proven stress-inducing game loops, such as time-management mechanics from the game, Cook, Serve, Delicious. This approach laid the groundwork for a minimalist yet addictive game loop that supports replayability.

TUTORIAL
​
Originally, the simplicity of the task mechanics resulted in the assumption that a tutorial was not required for the game. To our surprise, while the mechanics were simple to pick up, the additional variable of time pressure overwhelmed players.

​Playtesting revealed that players were only able to effectively pick up the mechanic after playing through the task at least once. After some ideating, we decided to implement an immersive tutorial that matched the theme of the game.

​Presented as a military training tape, task mechanics were taught to players, setting player expectations and increasing player confidence.

narrative design

NARRATIVE AMBIGUITY AND THE UNRELIABLE NARRATOR
​
The main character, Andrei. is inherently an unreliable narrator. This is an intentional design decision, representative of the real-world context where limited information is given to commanding officers regarding nuclear weapons during the Cold War.

​Real-life phrases used such as “Special Weapon” rather than “Nuclear Weapon” underscore the ambiguity of the situation, requiring players to piece together contextual information before making their final decision. With the revelation of nuclear weapons being at stake, it results in a twist that has the player reconsidering the weight of their actions.

designing for consequence

A critical part of the game experience is when the player makes the final decision of whether to launch the nuclear weapon, resulting in one of two possible endings. Initially, the transition from gameplay to this pivotal choice was immediate, offering players little to no time to reflect on their prior actions. While this design effectively sustained a sense of pressure throughout the experience, we found that players were often operating in a heightened state of stress. This frequently led to impulsive, emotionally charged decisions that bypassed deeper moral reflection. When we explored this further, players reported that they didn’t fully grasp the weight or consequences of their choice.

This led to the addition of a new scene, a voiceover sequence played over a black screen, giving players a brief moment of pause amid the escalating tension. Inspired by real quotes documented in historical archives, the voiceover pushes players to step into the perspective of Arkhipov and Petrov as they reflect on the counsel of those urging them to launch. At the end of the game is a statistical representation of player choices in the form of a pie chart, providing a striking representation regarding the possibility of a nuclear launch in real life.

retrospective

For more writing samples related to Till Midnight, please refer to my WRITING.

Designing a game that remained true to its real-life influences was a significant undertaking, requiring hours of careful research through declassified military files and historical interview segments about the events of each nuclear close call. However this led to greater historical accuracy for an authentic narrative experience.

The project was realised by our original team of eight members and a smaller, specialised team of four, focused solely on the Unity build. Throughout the transition, it was essential to maintain a clear direction and vision, an approach that allowed us to successfully streamline the handover process. The formation of a dedicated Unity team allowed us to concentrate on polishing the game's features.

Driven by a deep passion for games and art, our team approached this project with the belief that games can be more than entertainment. That they can be powerful tools for reflection and social impact. Till Midnight is a meaningful step toward our broader aspirations to create experiences that resonate with the human condition.

For me, this project reflects a personal passion for building something greater than myself. It shows how video games can spark empathy and raise awareness through storytelling and design.
