SUBTERFUGE

Spring 2023
Designer/Artist/Writer
Genre: Thriller, Mystery, Suspense

Synopsis: 
Subterfuge is a card game for 2-4 players. You play as the leader of a secret society. You have your goals and the means to accomplish them. However, you are not alone. Other forces conspire to derail your plans and claim dominance. Can you rise to the top while retaining your morality? Oh, and watch out for the moles!

a game of hidden agendas

The idea of secret societies have lingered in the public imagination for decades. Theories about the Illuminati weaving a New World Order or the moon landing being a hoax have become staples of pop culture. Prominent figures in government and celebrities, working in tandem for a shadowy agenda, are theorised as manipulating and pulling the invisible strings of society, allowing the elites to stay in power.

I was part of a project to design and manufacture the prototype of a card game with the above thematic concept.
Subterfuge final prototype

emotion by design
Subterfuge places players at the helm of a secret society, bringing with it the responsibility of fulfilling their organisation’s goals. Amid fierce competition from rival societies, each organisation is operating on its own agenda, driven by its own goals and ambitions.

​Through gameplay, players are given the ability to sabotage others or shore up their own defences, creating an unpredictable and competitive atmosphere of Subterfuge.

the challenge

The goal of this project was to design a card game that parodies the concept of secret societies and the moles within them, drawing on the lore of real-world secret societies and conspiracies to engage players.

HIGH LEVEL GOALS

1. Create a game system that allows players to commit acts of subterfuge, sabotage and sacrifice
2. Utilise a consistent, thematically relevant art style to captivate players
3. Include narrative references to real-world conspiracies and secret societies

MY ROLE

​Subterfuge began development in the spring of 2023, concluding after about 3 months. I took on the roles of Designer, Artist, and Writer, contributing to the design of the game's core systems, creating some of the card art, and writing the flavour text for various cards to help build a cohesive narrative.

project kickoff

We started by conducting playtesting research on several existing card games in the market to identify common systems, incorporating it into Subterfuge. For example, many games featured a draw phase followed by an action phase, during which players could play their cards. This was followed by research into various types of secret societies, hoaxes, and real-world conspiracies, an exciting process that called for me to put on my investigator cap. Eventually, we decided on four core types of cards that would help us to achieve our high-level goals.

MORALITY
Secret Societies are often portrayed as a counter to public good. As such, Morality works as a rare currency in these parts, and players will need to expend it wisely to gain an edge over their adversaries. Every turn, you gain 1 Morality point. Some cards replenish your Morality and Morality can be spent on clandestine activities.
ORGANISATION CARDS
Features a parody of real-world secret societies. Each one has a unique ability and a set number of starting Morality points. A random organisation can be drawn or selected at the start of the game.
CONSPIRACY CARDS
Features a parody of various real-world conspiracies and hoaxes. Costs Morality to play and must be placed on the bench. Each card comes with a special ability that lets you steal, draw, sacrifice, barter, or gamble your cards against others. Collect a set number of them to win as reflected in your Goal card.
GOAL CARDS
Your secret society’s overall goal, which you must keep hidden from other players. A random Goal card can be drawn or selected at the start of the game. It reflects the type of Conspiracy cards you need to have on the bench to win. The back features a Morality counter to track your Morality points.

homonyms and allusions

The design and theme of the cards feature a recurring motif: a mole, a character chosen for the homonym 'mole,' which refers both to the animal and to an infiltrator within an organisation. The double meaning of the word creates a strong allusion to the actions of secret societies, which are typically conducted undercover and in the dark.

the art deco movement

The Art Deco style was chosen for the cards because of the movement's heritage. Art Deco is commonly associated with powerful and wealthy elites, individuals commonly linked to secret societies. Characteristic of the movement, its signature ornate patterns and delicate gold trims are reflected in the art style of the cards and packaging in Subterfuge.

retrospective

Subterfuge went through several iterations, including playtesting with different focus groups and using quick paper prototypes to refine and balance the gameplay. Since this was a physical game, the team worked with vendors to evaluate the quality, weight (GSM), and texture of the paper used for the cards and rulebook. We also followed standard print design processes, such as adding proper bleed margins to the cards before printing.

Most of the game’s components were custom-designed, from card layouts to iconography. We also had the opportunity to explore 3D printing through an external vendor to produce custom tokens and coins, adding tactile depth and enhancing the thematic immersion of the game.

While the game could have relied less on RNG in some areas, the final version was balanced and fun to play. Developing a physical game from concept to prototype was a rewarding and educational process that gave us hands-on experience in both game design and physical product production.
