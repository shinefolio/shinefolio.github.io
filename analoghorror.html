ANALOG HORROR SERIES

Ongoing since Winter 2022
Solo Project/Writer/Editor
Genre: Analog Horror

Synopsis: 
A collection of standalone analog horror video projects. Themes include altered states of reality, liminal spaces and anomalous objects. Edited to evoke feelings of nostalgia or an odd sensation of familiarity through visuals that replicate vintage emergency broadcasts and public service announcements (PSA).

 All lore is original.

The Analog Horror Series is an ongoing project edited using Adobe Premier Pro as a means for me to practice my video editing skills. As it's a solo project, I'm responsible for writing, editing, sound and art assets. Third party assets were sourced for certain sounds and art.
GOOD LUCK CROW
The first entry in the series, Good Luck Crow is presented in the format of an Emergency Broadcast. It uses familiar childhood elements, such as a kids' mascot, and places them within a context that feels unsettlingly out of place. Exploring the nature of false memories and familiarity, it offers a psychologically disorienting perspective through a compelling question: Why do so many people find that liminal spaces feel familiar, even though they’ve ‘probably’ never been there before?
VEILPAINT PSA
The second entry in the series, Veilpaint is presented in the format of a Public Service Announcement. It uses a custom-created minimalistic, vector-based art style against a jarring blue background reminiscent of early cathode ray tube (CRT) displays. Focusing on the discovery of an anomalous object known as Veilpaint, it serves as a warning about the object's supernatural properties. Through the use of vector-based silhouettes, it effectively conveys the effects of Veilpaint on living and non-living entities.

Veilpaint PSA's editing style is inspired by the YouTube series Магнитошахтинская область
