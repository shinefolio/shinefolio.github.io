You’ve arrived in a digital space, host to a multiverse of projects. Here, you might find yourself in a dying submarine, awaiting the apocalypse. Another click teleports you into a pale, nostalgic reflection of an alternate reality. Everything within the span of the few minutes that you interact with this site.
When I was 8, I lost my beloved childhood stuffie. That was when I realised that all of our best memories remain only as stories. 

​I’m Tricia, a graduate of DigiPen Institute of Technology Singapore. My background in writing, film, user experience, and game design allows me to tackle a wide range of multidisciplinary projects.
Stories are at the heart of what I do. With a background in film and games, my designs are shaped by multimedia influences and inspired by Brenda Romero’s game design philosophy: “The mechanic is the message”. I'm passionate about impactful design and pushing the boundaries of creativity through innovation.
Metanarratives, post-reality and the future, scientific speculation, the physical and mental undercurrents of the human condition, social impact, aesthetics, reading, research, cosmic horror, astronomy, and why rain makes it easier to fall asleep, amongst others! :)
