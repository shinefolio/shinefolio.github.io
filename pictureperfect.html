PICTURE PERFECT

Spring 2025
Writer/Designer
Genre: Analog Horror, Mystery

Synopsis: 
Hey there player, come play Picture Perfect! Picture Perfect is a writing project for an alternate reality game (ARG). Play as Tara Lin, an artist with the ability to match and superimpose paintings onto reality, bringing the subjects of the painting to life. I can't wait till you discover Tara's story! ☆

an alternate reality game

An Alternate Reality Game (ARG) is an interactive story that uses elements of transmedia and the real world as a platform for storytelling and gameplay. It was popularised alongside the rise of internet Creepypastas and the style of analog horror, a subgenre of horror fiction and an offshoot of the found footage film genre. ARGs often feature meta-references and make use of games’ ability to break the fourth-wall as a literary device.

This was a solo project to write a complete script and story bible for an alternate reality game..

emotion by design

Picture Perfect unfolds like a slow-burn mystery, punctuated by moments of horror and revelation that slowly peel back to reveal the portrait of an artist that has lost control of their creation. As a story-puzzle game, it effectively puts the player in the role of an ​investigator, where the mechanics of the game are designed to encourage the player to piece together elements of story and solve puzzles.

Transmedia film elements and hidden messages are incorporated into the very fabric of its design, enriching the experience for attentive readers and players. With strong meta-narrative threads, the game's transmedia film segments draw inspiration from the found footage genre, popularised by films like The Blair Witch Project, blurring the line between fiction and reality.
Credits / The Blair Witch Project

the challenge

The goal of this project was to write, worldbuild, and develop the lore of an alternate reality game. As a solo endeavour, it was ambitious and served as an exercise in managing complex, layered narratives. Literary at its core, Picture Perfect weaves in elements of Animist philosoph​y and artificial consciousness to justify the events within the game. It is important that the story complements the game. Discovery of key story elements should feel natural and earned through gameplay and celebrated with key plot points in design.​

HIGH LEVEL GOALS

1. Ensure that the story complements the proposed gameplay and that plot reveals feel natural and well-earned
2. Develop a rich, multi-layered narrative ARG experience with subtle clues that hint at a larger story
3. Incorporate elements of philosophy and worldbuilding while avoiding jargon and unnecessary complexity

MY ROLE

Picture Perfect began its writing process in Fade In during the spring of 2025, before being transferred to Google Docs for easier viewing and collaboration. I took on the position of Lead Writer and Designer, working with gameplay mechanics proposed by a larger team. My responsibility was to develop the narrative direction as an offshoot of a broader game project, presenting it as a proposal for a potential creative direction.

crafting a multi-layered meta narrative

At the beginning of the project, I conducted research into existing ARGs and Creepypastas. From this, I gained a deep understanding of common ARG tropes, methods of delivery, and story structures. A defining characteristic of ARGs is their use of transmedia storytelling, an approach Picture Perfect adopts by weaving found footage film media into the gameplay.

Fourth-wall breaking, another hallmark of the genre, is incorporated into specific moments of the gameplay, such as the epilepsy end sequence. Most notably, Picture Perfect is presented diegetically. The game itself exists as a physical object within the narrative world. The developers are not external creators, but characters embedded in the broader story. This approach blurs the line between fiction and reality, creating a deliberate ambiguity between the game world and the real one.

“A”, “B” and “C” plot

Drawing from my background in film and television, Picture Perfect features three parallel storylines: A, B, and C. The main storyline (A) centres on the core mystery, where players uncover what happened to the original developer/protagonist in 1998. The B and C storylines build upon the plot while enriching and reframing the A story, offering additional depth and perspective.
"A" STORY
The primary storyline centred on the mystery surrounding Tara Lin, the original developer of Picture Perfect in 1998, the circumstances of her death, and the impact of her legacy.
"B" STORY
A subplot focused on the discovery of the Picture Perfect game in 2024, the students involved in its redevelopment and emulation, and the clues they uncovered from the original Picture Perfect game disc.
"C" STORY
A “runner” storyline that emerges through the player’s participation. While non-traditional in the sense of having direct interaction with the storyline or characters, the found footage gameplay of Picture Perfect encourages players to experience the narrative first-hand and form their own theories.

personification and symbolism

“Through cultural references and symbols, another layer of depth was added to the story".
╾╾╾╾╾╾╾
To convey philosophical concepts like animism to the layman, Picture Perfect uses object anthropomorphism in its dialogue, often by adding a prefix like 'Mr.' to personify them. This plays with audience expectations and structures dialogue like a loose parody of the story Goodnight Moon, as an allusion to the concept of assigning names and souls to non-human objects.

Additionally, a motif was associated with each character. Our protagonist, Tara, is represented with the motif of a star, connecting to her name’s Sanskrit meaning. Her animal character is designed to look like Hachiko the Akita, symbolic of the devotion attributed to the years of waiting that followed her mother’s disappearance. In comparison, Tara’s mother, Skye, is represented by the motif of the sky, acting as a poetic callback woven into hidden dialogue lines such as “The sky was lost an eternity ago”.

hiding in plain sight

Common tropes in ARGs often include the integration of cryptic ciphers, audio distortions, and hidden messages into gameplay. Picture Perfect uses the Baudot code cipher system, where in-game binaries can be translated into readable messages. Techniques such as spectrograms are also proposed for use in embedding hidden messages into the gameplay.

retrospective

ARGs are a relatively new genre, full of potential to engage players in unexpected, immersive, and innovative ways. It was this sense of creative possibility that excited me most for this writing project and fuelled the deep passion I have for pushing the boundaries of game design through bold experimentation and storytelling.

While this version of Picture Perfect remains a concept document, significant research and conceptualisation went into the final screenplay and story bible, including a detailed chronology of events and flowcharts to make the concept accessible to to readers unfamiliar with the ARG genre. The script and bible has undergone reader reviews, with adjustments made according to the feedback received.
