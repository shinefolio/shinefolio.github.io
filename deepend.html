DEEP END

​Winter 2023
3rd AD (On-set)/Production Assistant/Others
Genre: Crime/Thriller

Synopsis: 
​Deep End is a psychological crime thriller exploring the world of internet porn in Asia. In this series, "technology" is depicted as a tool that is both liberating and dangerous, filled with limitless information, images and videos that breed all kinds of ideas. 

​A commercial project completed during my internship from Summer 2021 to Spring 2022 with Akanga Film Asia and CJ ENM HK.

INTERNSHIP WITH AKANGA FILM ASIA
Deep End is a commercial project that I worked on during my internship with Akanga Film Asia, developed in collaboration with CJ ENM HK. During both the pre-production phase and on set, I had the opportunity to take on multiple roles including 3rd Assistant Director (on-set), Assistant Script Supervisor, Production Assistant, and Locations Assistant. It was an enriching experience that gave me an insight into the fast-paced demands of commercial filmmaking.

​This opportunity also allowed me to collaborate with and learn from many talented professionals and actors, which has been invaluable in shaping my passion for films and filmmaking.
ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
As a 3rd Assistant Director (on-set), I was responsible for building rapport with key talent, ensuring they were ready on schedule, cueing and directing background actors (extras) by explaining key script moments to them.

As an Assistant Script Supervisor, I supported the meticulous process of maintaining scene continuity by keeping a visual reference record and tracking shot numbers and usable takes.
​
As a Production and Locations Assistant, I handled tasks related to location scouting and project coordination.
